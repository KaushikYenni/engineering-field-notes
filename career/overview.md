# My path so far

I've spent about six years in software engineering, working on backend
systems, distributed services, and data-heavy products. The work I remember
best is often close to production: figuring out why a system stopped making
progress or making a long-running job more resilient.

Most recently, I was a Software Engineer 2 at Microsoft, working on Purview
Insider Risk Management. Before that I worked at Expedia Group, Sandvine, and
Accolite. My projects have ranged from insurance shopping and subscriber
connectivity to a global Elasticsearch-to-Kusto migration and investigation
data retention.

I graduated with a B.Tech. in Electronics and Communication from IIT
Bhubaneswar in 2020. Here is [where I've worked](timeline.md), and here are
[some stories from production](../stories/README.md).
