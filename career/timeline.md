# Where I've worked

I started at [Accolite](../companies/previous-companies/README.md) as a
Software Engineer in September 2020 and stayed through December. One project
I remember from that short stint was automating distributed image
synchronization across multi-region server clusters.

From January 2021 to May 2022, I was a Software Engineer at [Sandvine](../companies/previous-companies/README.md).
I led the release of a feature for real-time detection and diagnosis of
subscriber connectivity issues.

I joined [Expedia Group](../companies/previous-companies/README.md) as a
Software Development Engineer II in May 2022. I worked on insurance products:
entry points for Hotels.com, shopping
orchestration across checkout and post-purchase flows, SOX audits, and
separating post-purchase and PCI functionality from an existing service. In March 2025, I was promoted to 
Software Development Engineer III. In that role, I worked on an insurance-product rule engine, adding 
new operators and nested conditions.


In May 2025 I joined [Microsoft](../companies/microsoft/README.md) as a
Software Engineer 2 on Purview Insider Risk Management. The work I remember
most includes the Elasticsearch-to-Kusto
migration, data lifecycle management for active investigations, and reducing
noise from temporary-file activity.
